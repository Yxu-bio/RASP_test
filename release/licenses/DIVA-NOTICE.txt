DIVA 1.1 redistribution and attribution notice
================================================

DIVA was developed by Fredrik Ronquist, Department of Systematic Zoology,
Evolutionary Biology Centre, Uppsala University.

The DIVA 1.1 User's Manual states that the program and manual may be copied and
distributed freely provided that the source of results or ideas is cited. It
also states that the source code may be copied, modified, and recompiled.

RASP distributes a legacy RASP-modified DIVA executable for DIVA and S-DIVA
compatibility. Results should cite DIVA and the applicable RASP publications.

Original project download:
https://sourceforge.net/projects/diva/

Manual citation:
Ronquist, F. 1996. DIVA version 1.1. Computer program and manual.

Method citation:
Ronquist, F. 1997. Dispersal-vicariance analysis: a new approach to the
quantification of historical biogeography. Systematic Biology 46:195-203.
